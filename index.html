My Individualism in Feminine Tech

How I've used pink to prove points

Guess the Percentage

What is the percentage of women in the STEM workforce?

Your guess: 50%

The actual answer is 26%

This statistic is much lower than it should be. For every 2 men employed in tech, only 1 woman is. Despite this, women were some of the first people to write computer codes. People like Ada Lovelace or Grace Hopper are incredibly important figures in the CIS world, yet women don't receice nearly enough representation.

Thoreau's Ideas

At least, the ones that stuck out to me. He did say to not stretch the seams of the coat. ¯\_(ツ)_/¯

01

Be yourself!

"If a man does not keep pace with his companions, perhaps it is because he hears a different drummer." -Thoreau's Individualism

Do what YOU want to do and what YOU are meant to do. Don't base who you are on what you supposedly "should" be.

02

Comparison will KILL you

"Shall a man go and hang himself because he belongs to the race of pygmies, and not be the biggest pygmy that he can?" -Thoreau's Individualism

Comparing yourself will stop you from being the best you can be. Even if your best is no where near what the overall best is.

03

Find Finer Fruits

So many people get caught in cycles. Eat, work, sleep, repeat. Your finer fruits are your little joys.

"If they had not been overcome with drowsiness, they would have preformed something." -Thoreau's Simplicity

04

Sometimes inheriting things can be a burden

"...my townsmen, whose misfortune it is to have inherited farms, houses, barns, cattle, and farming tools; for those are more easily aquired than got rid of." -Thoreau's Walden

You inherit a farm, this is great! But did you actually want this? Now you have much more work to do...
