My Individualism in Feminine Tech

How I've been able to see Thoreau's ideas in my own life, as I navigate the tech world as a woman.

Guess the Percentage

What is the percentage of women in the STEM workforce?

Your guess: 50%

The actual answer is 26%

This statistic is much lower than it should be. For every 2 men employed in tech, only 1 woman is. Despite this, women were some of the first people to write computer codes. People like Ada Lovelace or Grace Hopper are incredibly important figures in the CIS world, yet women don't receice nearly enough representation.

Thoreau's Ideas

At least, the ones that stuck out to me. He did say to not stretch the seams of the coat. ¯\_(ツ)_/¯

01

Be yourself!

"If a man does not keep pace with his companions, perhaps it is because he hears a different drummer." -Thoreau's Individualism

Do what YOU want to do and what YOU are meant to do. Don't base who you are on what you supposedly "should" be.

02

Comparison will KILL you

"Shall a man go and hang himself because he belongs to the race of pygmies, and not be the biggest pygmy that he can?" -Thoreau's Individualism

Comparing yourself will stop you from being the best you can be. Even if your best is no where near what the overall best is.

03

Find Finer Fruits

So many people get caught in cycles. Eat, work, sleep, repeat. Your finer fruits are your little joys.

"If they had not been overcome with drowsiness, they would have preformed something." -Thoreau's Simplicity

04

Sometimes inheriting things can be a burden

"...my townsmen, whose misfortune it is to have inherited farms, houses, barns, cattle, and farming tools; for those are more easily aquired than got rid of." -Thoreau's Walden

You inherit a farm, this is great! But did you actually want this? Now you have much more work to do...

Idea 1. Be Yourself

As you can see from this slideshow, I love pink. So many sites use the same formulas:

GREY GREY GREY

This gets SOOOOOOO boring. This is why I love pink. It is a color that is not used often, and it is a color that is fun.

Thoreau says to do what you want to do, and not what everone else is doing. My website works just as well as yours, but mine is PINK!

Going back to the statistic at the beginning, I am also able to use the color pink to prove a point. Many women working in STEM experience a "bro" culture. By making the things I create pink, I am able to show that a woman can create something just as good as a man. My pink specifically says, "I'm a woman, and I am just as capable."

Idea 2. Comparison will KILL you

Okay, maybe not literally kill you, but it will stop you from being the best you can be.

I enjoy hardware. I'll try to wire things up, design pcbs, but at the end of the day, they're only okay.

Now, one of my friends is incredible at hardware. Every time I see something they built, I'm just amazed. And then I try to build something myself, and I feel like it's really bad compared to theirs.

But that's it! The word: I compared myself to them.

The goal of my building is to create something I enjoy, and to enjoy the process. At the end of the day, my ability is how it is, so why don't I spend my time just enjoying the process of creating?

Idea 3. Find Finer Fruits

I'm currently going through junior year. I have TONS of homework, and I've been falling into the same patterns:

Wake up, go to school, do homework, sleep, repeat.

I'm not the only one who feels like this, and it definitely isn't easy. Thoreau says to try and find finer fruits thoughout your life to avoid just trudging through the same cycles.

For me, my finer fruits are things like programming, pink things, and finding time to spend with friends. You finer fruits don't need to be extravagant. I'm not telling you to pick up a 20 step skincare routine, or drop $1000 on a new hobby, but look at what you have and what you enjoy and try to find releif in the little things you enjoy.

Idea 4. Sometimes inheriting things can be a burden

I have a lot of inherited responsibilities and expectations. Wether it's from parents, teachers, friends or society, I feel like I have to uphold certain ideas.

Going back to those boring grey websites, sometimes it feels like I have to make mine that exact same way. Sometimes I even feel like I should just do something else other than programming purely because of how male dominated the feild is.

I'm the only girl in my computer class, and one of two in a big electronics class.I certainly don't fit into those areas. This can be really hard, and sometimes it even gives me more pressure because I feel like I have to earn my spot in these places.

The previous slides are all about doing what YOU want to do, not what everyone else wants. Thoreau tells you to take all expectations and decide if it's what YOU want. Don't let uneccesary burdens weigh you down.

You deserve to be in the spaces you want to be in.
